🎮 How to Play & Core Mechanics
Write Code (Lines of Code):

Click on the laptop to generate Lines of Code (LoC) — your main in-game currency.

Unlock auto-upgrades like Auto-Linters, AI Copilot, Freelancers, and CI/CD pipelines to generate code passively.

Take Care of the Cat:

Mochi has Hunger and Mood meters.

If you ignore your cat for too long, he will meow, jump onto your keyboard, and block you from typing until you pet or feed him.

Upgrade Your Setup:

Level up your cozy studio: brew specialty V60 pour-over coffee, upgrade to an ultrawide monitor, get a mechanical keyboard, and build the ultimate dev workspace.

Atmosphere & Details:

Cozy pixel-style interior featuring a scenic window with dynamic day/night cycles and moving weather/clouds.

Web Audio API synthesized sound effects: mechanical key clicks, cat purrs/meows, coffee brewing, and ambient sounds without relying on external assets.
